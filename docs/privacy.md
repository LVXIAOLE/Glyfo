# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2 September 2026**

Glyfo does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your data. It has no network code at all —
no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. Everything the
app does happens on your PC.

## What Glyfo processes

To do its job, Glyfo reads:

- **Images you give it** — a region of the screen you capture with the shortcut, a file you open
  or drag in, an image you paste, or an image shared to Glyfo from another app.
- **The text it recognises in those images**, including QR code and barcode contents.
- **Your clipboard**, when you paste an image into Glyfo or copy recognised text out of it.

Glyfo only captures the screen at the moment you ask it to. It does not watch, record, or
monitor your screen in the background.

## Where that data goes

Nowhere. Text recognition uses the OCR engine built into Windows. On Copilot+ PCs, the improved
recogniser and the translation feature use the AI models that ship with Windows and run on the
device's own hardware. Read-aloud uses the speech voices installed in Windows. None of these send
your images or text off the device, and Glyfo makes no internet connections of its own.

## What Glyfo stores on your PC

| What | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your preferences (language, OCR options, shortcut, toggles) | Windows app settings for Glyfo, on this PC only | So the app remembers how you set it up. These are local settings and are not synced to your Microsoft account. |
| Temporary copies of images opened from other apps | `%TEMP%\Glyfo\` | So an image shared or opened from another app survives long enough to be recognised. |
| A diagnostic log of app events and errors | `%TEMP%\Glyfo.log` | To make faults reportable. It records events such as "the app started" or "a file was opened", plus error messages. It does not contain your images or the text Glyfo recognised. |

You can delete the two `%TEMP%` items at any time; Glyfo recreates them as needed. Uninstalling
Glyfo removes its settings.

## Children

Glyfo is a general-purpose utility. It is not directed at children and collects nothing from
anyone.
